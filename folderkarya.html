  • Date: 15 August, 2023

Gak nyangka banget tim kita, Tim Folder Karya dari Universitas Amikom Yogyakarta, bisa nembus jajaran 10 Peserta Terbaik Kompetisi Elevator Pitch PHR VR Challenge 2023! Perjuangan dari awal nyiapin konsep ide Virtual Reality, latihan pitching berkali-kali biar nggak belibet pas ngomong di depan juri, sampai deg-degan nungguin pengumuman bener-bener ngasih pengalaman seru yang nggak bakal dilupain. Walaupun banyak drama kecil pas latihan, kerja sama tim yang solid bener-bener ngebantu kita buat ngejawabin semua tantangan dari Pertamina Hulu Rokan ini.

Pengalaman kemarin ngebuktiin kalau anak kuliahan juga bisa andil dan kasih ide kreatif buat ngembangin teknologi masa depan. Makasih banyak buat semua pihak, dosen, dan tentunya rekan satu tim yang udah gas terus berjuang bareng dari nol sampai dapet panggung keren ini. Semoga pencapaian ini jadi langkah awal buat kita terus bikin inovasi-inovasi gokil lainnya yang bermanfaat buat banyak orang. Let's keep building the future! 🕶️️🚀🔥.

I genuinely can't believe that our team, Folder Karya Team from Universitas Amikom Yogyakarta, made it into the top 10 Best Participants of the PHR VR Challenge 2023 Elevator Pitch Competition! The struggle from initially preparing the Virtual Reality concept, practicing the pitch over and over so we wouldn't stutter in front of the judges, to nervously waiting for the results gave us an unforgettable experience. Even with all the little dramas during practice, our solid teamwork truly helped us tackle all the challenges from Pertamina Hulu Rokan.

That experience proved that college students can also step up and contribute creative ideas for developing future technology. Huge thanks to everyone, our lecturers, and of course my teammates who kept pushing through together from scratch up to this awesome stage. Hopefully, this achievement becomes the starting point for us to keep creating other crazy innovations that benefit many people. Let's keep building the future! 🕶️🚀🔥
